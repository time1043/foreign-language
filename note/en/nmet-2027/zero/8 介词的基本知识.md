# 介词短语：介词+名词/代词

- 一组词：以介词开始
- 包括一个名词或代词，也就是介词的宾语
- 介词短语主要内容：什么时间、什么地点、什么方式

```markdown
I eat lunch after my class. 什么时间
The Summer Palace is in Beijing. 什么地点
He likes to work by himself. 什么方式
```

# 时间短语

| 介词 | 用法                     | 例句                                                                                                                                   |
| :--- | :----------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| at   | 钟表时间、正午/午夜      | Class begins at 8 a.m. I eat lunch at noon.                                                                                            |
| on   | 星期几、具体日期         | The city collects trash on Mondays. School is closed on July 4th.                                                                      |
| in   | 月份、年、季节、更长时间 | Spring begins in March. A new century began in 2000. My family goes on vacation in the summer. Cellphones became popular in the 1990s. |

## 注意

**1、同时使用时间短语和地点短语：先地点，后时间**

```markdown
I go to my office at 7 a.m.
→ I go to my office at 7 a.m.

He reads his book. He studies in the library.
→ He studies in the library at 8 o'clock.
```

**2、介词短语放在句首时，需要用逗号断开**

```markdown
In Japan, people drive on the left side of the road.
→ People drive on the left side of the road in Japan.

In April, Japanese students start school.
→ Japanese students start school in April.
```

## 注意：常见错误

```markdown
介词错误 Miami and Orlando are on Florida. → Miami and Orlando are in Florida.
介词缺失 I like to listen to music my car. → I like to listen to music in my car.
地点时间顺序错误 Ed goes on Tuesdays and Thursdays to his classes.
→ Ed goes to his classes on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
缺少逗号 On the weekends I go to the beach.
→ On the weekends, I go to the beach.
```

## 练习：将介词短语移到句首，写成句子

```markdown
1. My sister has English class on Mondays.
   On Mondays, my sister has English class.
2. Lynn, Jane, and Karen usually take bus 28 on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
   On Tuesdays and Thursdays, Lynn, Jane, and Karen usually take bus 28.
3. You can see a business map of downtown on page 128.
   On page 128, you can see a business map of downtown.
4. Adjectives often come before nouns in English.
   In English, adjectives often come before nouns.
5. Kevin and I have a very important meeting at 7:00 tonight.
   At 7:00 tonight, Kevin and I have a very important meeting.
6. You need a photo ID for many reasons.
   For many reasons, you need a photo ID.
7. My mother buys vegetables at the supermarket on Saturday mornings.
   On Saturday mornings, my mother buys vegetables at the supermarket.
8. Melissa and I study at the library in the afternoon.
   In the afternoon, Melissa and I study at the library.
9. Students eat lunch in the cafeteria from noon to 1 p.m.
   From noon to 1 p.m., students eat lunch in the cafeteria.
10. We plan to move to another apartment in November.
    In November, we plan to move to another apartment.
```

**3、介词短语放在句中时，用于描述前面的名词，不影响动词变化**

```markdown
The reason for this decision is clear.
The reason for these decisions is clear.
```
