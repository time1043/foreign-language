# 什么是形容词 What Is an Adjective?

- 形容词是给出人物、事物或地点相关信息的词

| 有描述类型 | 词举例                                                       | 句举例                                                        |
| :--------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 描述形容词 | black, happy, bad, big, hot, difficult, pretty, empty, ready | Black clouds mean bad weather. Learning English is difficult. |
| 物主形容词 | my, your, his, her, its, our, their                          | My car is next to your house.                                 |
| 指示形容词 | this, that, these, those, such                               | Those students need these pens.                               |
| 数词量词   | some, many, every, six, ten                                  | Many students have three classes.                             |
| 名词       | 所有名词                                                     | My math class is difficult. Mr. Miller works in a pet store.  |

# 描述形容词

## 1、描述形容词的基本用法

- 描述形容词用于描述名词，可以放在**名词前面**，或放在 **be 动词后面**

```markdown
We have a new clock. The clock is new.
```

- 描述形容词不受名词单复数影响

```markdown
We have a new clock. We have two new clocks.
```

## 注意：常见错误

```markdown
形容词位置错误 I have a car red. → I have a red car.
形容词没有单复数 She has ten reds apples. → She has ten red apples.
```

# 描述形容词变成副词：重要考点

## 基本变化：在形容词末尾加 -y

| 形容词  | 副词      | 形容词 | 副词     |
| :------ | :-------- | :----- | :------- |
| careful | carefully | quick  | quickly  |
| clear   | clearly   | quiet  | quietly  |
| current | currently | slow   | slowly   |
| sad     | sadly     | sudden | suddenly |

## 以 y 结尾且读音为 /i/ 的形容词，去 y 改 i 再加 -ly

## 以 y 结尾且读音为 /ai/ 的形容词，直接加 -ly

| 形容词 | 副词    | 形容词 | 副词    |
| :----- | :------ | :----- | :------ |
| happy  | happily | heavy  | heavily |
| angry  | angrily | busy   | busily  |
| easy   | easily  | noisy  | noisily |
| dry    | dryly   | shy    | shyly   |

## 以 ic 结尾的词，加 -ally（public 例外）

| 形容词    | 副词          | 形容词     | 副词           |
| :-------- | :------------ | :--------- | :------------- |
| specific  | specifically  | scientific | scientifically |
| basic     | basically     | energetic  | energetically  |
| automatic | automatically | classic    | classically    |
| economic  | economically  | public     | publicly       |

## 其它变化规则

- 大多数辅音字母加 e 结尾的形容词，直接加 -ly
- 少数以 e 结尾的形容词，去掉 e 再加 -ly
- 大多数元音字母加 e 结尾的形容词，直接加 -ly
- 辅音字母加 ie 结尾的形容词，去掉 e 加 -y
- 以 ll 结尾的形容词，直接加 y

# 物主形容词

- 物主形容词体现某人拥有某物

| 主语 | 物主形容词 | 例句                                            |
| :--- | :--------- | :---------------------------------------------- |
| I    | my         | I have a book. My book is interesting.          |
| you  | your       | You have a car. Your car is white.              |
| he   | his        | He has a watch. His watch is gold.              |
| she  | her        | She has a watch. Her watch is silver.           |
| it   | its        | It has a leg. Its leg is short.                 |
| we   | our        | We have books. Our books are heavy.             |
| they | their      | They have watches. Their watches are expensive. |

## 注意：常见错误

```markdown
性别错误 Mary has her doctor appointment today.
形式错误 They have their laptops.
形式错误 I do not have my book.
```

# 指向形容词

- 指向形容词用于指出特定的名词
- 指向形容词有单复数变化

| 单数                                | 复数                      |
| :---------------------------------- | :------------------------ |
| 距离说话者近 This passport is blue. | These passports are blue. |
| 距离说话者远 That passport is red.  | Those passports are red.  |

## 注意：常见错误

```markdown
单复数错误 This lesson is simple.
语序错误 These books are very good.
```

## 练习：选择正确的指向形容词

```markdown
1. I can't answer (this / these) exam questions.
2. (That / Those) glass is empty.
3. Who is (this / that) man over there?
4. (This / These) students are from Colombia.
5. Can you help me with (this / these) question?
6. (This / These) map is the best for your class.
7. The teacher can explain (that / those) grammar lesson again.
8. (That / Those) eggs are for your lunch tomorrow.
```

# 名词作形容词

- 使用名词作形容词时，第一个名词描述第二个名词
- 名词作形容词时必须为单数

```markdown
my Tuesday class = my class on Tuesday
a math test = a test about math
a shoe store = a store that sells shoes
```

## 练习：使用名词作形容词改写句子

```markdown

   My teacher can answer this math question.

   Please come to the Monday morning meeting.

   I need some tennis shoes.

   I walk to the bus station every morning.
7. This is a book. The book is about history.
   This is a history book.
8. Let's meet at the shop. The shop sells coffee.
   Let's meet at the coffee shop.
9. Their garden is big. The garden has vegetables.
   Their vegetable garden is big.
```
