# 现在进行时句子里时间副词的位置

## 1、`today, now, nowadays, at this time, these days, this week, this year` 等，可以放在句首或句尾。多数时间短语放在句首需要逗号断开

```markdown
Wildlife biologists **are tracking** bears _this year_.
_This year_, wildlife biologists **are tracking** bears.
```

## 2、类似 `currently` 或 `presently` 等副词，可以放在句首、句中和句尾。在句中时，放在两个动词中间

```markdown
_Presently_, the support rate of the government is growing.
The highway department **is** _currently_ **building** fences to protect deer.
```

## 3、`still` 放在两个动词中间

```markdown
For economic reasons, many people **are** _still_ **living** without health care.
```

# 现在进行时的作用

## 1、描述某个动作正在发生，可以是在这一刻，也可以是在现在这一段时间

```markdown
The president **is visiting** two foreign countries _this week_.
I **am looking** forward to seeing you in the university campus.
```

- https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1MY4y1P7Jj?t=5321.6&p=15
- 关键是站在哪个位置，描述的这个事件；this week 是现在进行时的标志 🙅 时间副词和时态并非一一对应
- The president **is visiting** two foreign countries _this week_.
- The president **visited** two foreign countries _this week_.
- The president **will visit** two foreign countries _this week_.

- https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1MY4y1P7Jj?t=5555.6&p=15
