# 什么是现在进行时 What is Present Progressive?

- 现在进行时表示**现在**正在发生的动作
- 狭义现在：准确的在这一刻（即时）
- 模糊的现在这一段（暂时）

```markdown
We are watching TV now.
I am working this week.
Michael usually drives to school, but he is riding his bike today.
It rained a lot last night, but the sun is shining this morning.
```

# 现在进行时的构成

- 现在进行时：be 动词+动词-ing 形式（am/is/are+doing）

| 主语        | be 动词肯定 | be 动词否定 | 动词+ing 形式 | 其它信息         |
| :---------- | :---------- | :---------- | :------------ | :--------------- |
| I           | am          | am not      | working       | right now.       |
| He / She / It | is        | is not      | eating        | dinner now.      |
| We / You / They | are    | are not     | making        | our trip today.  |
|             |             |             | planning      |                  |

# 动词-ing 形式的变化：动词+ing

## 1、动词未尾直接加 -ing

```markdown

No one is eating in the cafeterias right now.
check – checking    help – helping    call – calling
look – looking      rain – raining    listen – listening
answer – answering  talk – talking
```

## 2、以字母 e 结尾的动词，去 e 加 -ing；以字母 ee 结尾的动词，直接加 -ing

```markdown
Many people are taking the train because of the snow.
I am baking a cake for my friend's birthday.
move – moving    like – liking    close – closing
waste – wasting  hope – hoping    agree – agreeing
see – seeing     free – freeing
```

## 3、以重读闭音节辅音元音辅音结尾的动词，双写最后一个字母，再加 -ing


## 注意：常见错误

**时态错误**

```markdown
Jessica is eating lunch at 12:30 every day.
→ Jessica eats lunch at 12:30 every day.

Jessica eats lunch right now. → Jessica is eating lunch right now.
```

**现在进行时结构不完整**

```markdown
Now we planning our vacation. → Now we are planning our vacation.
Ali and I are cook spaghetti now. → Ali and I are cooking spaghetti now.
```

**doing 形态错误**

```markdown
I am writing an email now. → I am writing an email now.
```

**有些动词不使用现在进行时**

```markdown
The tourists are needing directions. → The tourists need directions.
```

**句子啰嗦**

```markdown
They are eating, and they are drinking. → They are eating and drinking.
```

# 现在进行时句子里时间副词的位置

## 1、today, now, nowadays, at this time, these days, this week, this year 等，可以放在句首或句尾。多数时间短语放在句首需要逗号断开

```markdown
Wildlife biologists are tracking bears this year.
This year, wildlife biologists are tracking bears.
```

## 2、类似 currently 或 presently 等副词，可以放在句首、句中和句尾，在句中时，放在两个动词中间

```markdown
Presently, the support rate of the government is growing.
The highway department is currently building fences to protect deer.
```

## 3、still 放在两个动词中间

```markdown
For economic reasons, many people are still living without health care.
```

# 现在进行时的作用

## 1、描述某个动作正在发生，可以是在这一刻，也可以是在现在这一段时间

```markdown
The president is visiting two foreign countries this week.
I am looking forward to seeing you in the university campus.
```

## 2、表示现在的情况正在发生变化

```markdown
Since the cost of parking is increasing, more and more people are sharing rides to work and school.
People are drinking more coffee than ever before.
```

## 3、强调动作反复发生，经常和 always 或 still 连用，有抱怨或责备的语气

```markdown
You are always making promises.
Most places always have Internet security, but viruses are still getting into computer systems.
```

**注意：一般现在时用于表示习惯、常规和日常情况（发生许多次的动作、一直都这样的动作）**
