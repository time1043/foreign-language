# 什么是定语从句 What is an Adjective Clause?

- 形容词：给出人物、事物或地点相关信息的词
- 英语句子：一组词，有完整含义，有主语，有动词
- 从句：由引导词开始的句子，不能脱离主句独立存在
- 定语从句就是一个从句作为形容词，由 **who, which, that, whom** 引导
- 定语从句在它要描述的名词、代词或名词短语后面
- 引导词对应定语从句要描述的名词，名词短语或代词

# 定语从句就是形容词从句

| 两个简单句                                     | 定语从句                                           |
| :--------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| Tennis has many special terms. 网球里有很多术语 | Tennis has many special terms **that** are hard to discuss the game. 这些术语适于描述这项运动 |
| Some people do not know the meaning of these terms. 有些人不知道这些术语的含义 | Some people **who** are not familiar with tennis do not know the meaning of these terms. 有些不熟悉网球的人不知道这些术语的含义 |

# 定语从句的构成

1. 找到两句话的共同名词
2. 保留主句的名词，去掉从句的名词
3. 用引导词把从句安在主句名词的后面
4. 引导词的基本原则：人使用 that/who/whom，物使用 that/which

```markdown
Joe met a man.  The man is very famous.
主句             从句
Joe met a man that is very famous.
乔遇到了一个人。这个人非常有名。
乔遇到了一个非常有名的人。
```

```markdown
The subject is science.  This subject was the hardest.
主句                     从句
The subject that was the hardest is science.
这个学科是科学。这个学科是最难的
这个最难的学科是科学。
```

```markdown
They are meeting the teacher.  Everyone likes the teacher.
They are meeting the teacher that everyone likes.
他们在和那个老师见面，每个人都会喜欢那个老师。
他们在和那个每个人都喜欢的老师见面。
```

```markdown
The girl is a new student.   I met her in the hallway.
The girl that I met in the hallway is a new student.
那个女孩是一个新学生。我在走廊里遇到了他。
那个我在走廊里遇到的女孩是一个学生。
```

## 练习：把两个句子使用定语从句合并

```markdown
1. A coach is a person. This person trains athletes.
   A coach is a person that trains athletes.
2. Romansch is a language. It comes from Latin.
   Romansch is a language that comes from Latin.
3. Elon Musk is a businessman. He wants to make space travel easier and more common.
   Elon Musk is a businessman that wants to make space travel easier and more common.
4. The Titanic was a ship. It sank in the North Atlantic Ocean in 1912.
   The Titanic was a ship that sank in the North Atlantic Ocean in 1912.
5. The movie was Black Panther. We saw this movie at the theater last night.
   The movie that we saw at the theater last night was Black Panther.
6. The dream was strange. I had this dream last night.
   The dream that I had last night was strange.
7. The homework assignment was difficult. The grammar teacher gave us the homework assignment.
   The homework assignment that the grammar teacher gave us was difficult.
8. The famous actress was very friendly. I saw this actress at the restaurant.
   The famous actress that I saw at the restaurant was very friendly.
```

# 定语从句的引导词

## 1、引导词在从句中作主语

| 引导词         | 用法         | 例句                                           |
| :------------- | :----------- | :--------------------------------------------- |
| who 或 that    | 对应人       | Middle-aged people **who** exercise tend to have fewer severe health problems later in life. |
| which 或 that  | 对应物       | More consumers are buying vehicles **that** use electricity. |
| 有逗号时只能用 who  | 对应人 | Nelson Mandela, **who** died in 2013, was president of South Africa from 1994 to 1999. |
| 有逗号时只能用 which | 对应物 | The Nissan Leaf, **which** was introduced in 2010, is an all-electric car. |

## 2、引导词在从句中作宾语（可以省略）

| 引导词         | 用法         | 例句                                           |
| :------------- | :----------- | :--------------------------------------------- |
| whom 或 that   | 对应人       | This is the man **whom** you met in the school building yesterday. |
| which 或 that  | 对应物       | Scientists are developing a vitamin **that** people will take only once a week. |

## 3、引导词在从句中作介词的宾语

| 引导词           | 用法         | 例句                                           |
| :--------------- | :----------- | :--------------------------------------------- |
| 介词+whom 对应人 |              | You should make eye contact with the person **to whom** you are talking. |
| 介词+which 对应物 |              | They conducted a study in **which** they surveyed young coffee drinkers. |

# 定语从句引导词的简单原则

```
无逗号 that/who人  that/which 物
有逗号 who 人      which 物
介词+whom 人      介词+which 物
```

```markdown

2. Rosa Parks is the woman (who / whom) some call "the first lady of civil rights."
3. In 1961, the German Democratic Republic built a wall (that / whom) divided the city of Berlin into two cities.
4. Some people (whom / who) get migraines started getting them when they were very young children.

8. The new lecturer was not aware that the person (whom / with / who) he shared an office was a concert pianist.
```

## 注意：主句和定语从句的划分原则

```markdown
Scientists are conducting an experiment that will change people's life. 从句为宾语服务
Tallahassee, which is in north Florida, is the state's capital. 从句为主语服务，有逗号断开
The city that we studied was ancient. 从句为主语服务，无逗号断开（找到第二个没有关系的动词断开）
```

## 定语从句常见错误：引导词使用错误

**1、引导词注意区分人和物**

```markdown
Because of housing shortages in Shanghai, single Chinese people which who that are from other cities in China are not allowed to buy property there.
```

**2、逗号后面不能用 that**

```markdown
Amphibians, that which are animals that can breathe on land and in the water, are cold-blooded.
```

**3、引导词不能缺失**

```markdown
The students who that improved the most were the ones from bilingual families.
```

## 练习：填入正确的引导词

2. Clouds are simply a gathering of very small droplets of water or crystals of ice which/that are so light that they can float in the air.
3. Great managers are people that/who can delegate, encourage, and give credit to those employees that/who deserve it.

# 定语从句常见错误：动词单复数错误

- 从句的动词要和被修饰的名词保持 **"主语动词一致"**

```markdown
The person who were honored at the ceremony started one of the biggest companies in Silicon Valley.
Tourists who visits visit Beijing's Forbidden City can see the inside of the imperial palace.
The number of women who go to school in Afghanistan have has increased by over 30 percent since 2002.
```

**the number of + 名词 名词的数量 主语是 the number**
**a number of + 名词 许多名词 主语是名词**
**a xxx of + 名词 量词**
**a group of tourists 一群游客**

## 练习：选择正确的动词单复数

```markdown
1. Cars that (runs / run) on electricity (saves / save) gas and are highly praised by environmentalists.
2. Researchers who (is / are) trying to find a cure for cancer sometimes (works / work) at large research universities such as Stanford, Yale, or Johns Hopkins.
3. The Nobel Prizes that (is / are) awarded each year (is / are) for physics, chemistry, medicine, and literature.
4. A panda that (lives / live) in a zoo (has / have) a longer life span than one in the wild.
5. Birds that do not (travel / travels) south (is / are) able to find enough food throughout the year.
6. Teachers who (flip / flips) their classrooms (has / have) moved traditional lectures out of the classroom.
```

# 定语从句的基本作用

## 1、合并短句

```markdown
Redwood trees grow mainly on the Pacific coast from Oregon to California.
Redwood trees are the tallest trees in the world.
Redwood trees, which are the tallest trees in the world, grow mainly on the Pacific coast from Oregon to California.
```

## 2、描述重要信息（没有用逗号断开）

```markdown
The bark of the redwood tree contains chemicals that protect the tree from insects.
```

## 3、补充其它信息（可以用逗号断开）

```markdown
The coast redwood tree, which is the same height as a 37-story building, is the tallest type of redwood tree.
```
