# 什么是定语从句 What is an Adjective Clause?

- 形容词：给出人物、事物或地点相关信息的词
- 英语句子：一组词，有完整含义，有主语，有动词
- 从句：由引导词开始的句子，不能脱离主句独立存在

- **定语从句**就是一个从句作为形容词，由 **who, which, that, whom** 引导
- 定语从句在它要**描述的名词、代词或名词短语**后面
- **引导词**对应定语从句要**描述的名词，名词短语或代词**
- 定语从句就是**形容词从句**

| 两个简单句                                                                                                     | 定语从句                                                                                                   |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tennis **has** many _special terms_. <br>_They_ **are used** to discuss the game.                              | Tennis **has** many _special terms_ ==that== **are used** to discuss the game.                             |
| _Some people_ **do not know** the meaning of these special terms. <br>_They_ **are not familiar** with tennis. | _Some people_ ==who== **are not familiar** with tennis **do not know** the meaning of these special terms. |

```markdown
网球里有许多术语。
这些术语用于描述这项运动。
=> 网球里有许多 _用于描述这项运动的术语_。

有些人不知道这些术语的含义。
他们不熟悉网球。
=> 有些 _不熟悉网球的人_ 不知道这些术语的含义。

我有一个母亲。

=> 我有一个 _爱我的母亲_。

我买了一本书。
这本书讲述英国历史在过去200年间的变迁。
=> 我买了一本 _讲述英国历史在过去200年间的变迁的书_。
```

## 定语从句的构成

1. 找到两句话的共同名词
2. 保留主句的名词，去掉从句的名词
3. 用引导词把从句安在主句名词的后面
4. 引导词的基本原则：人使用 that/who/whom，物使用 that/which

```markdown
Joe met a man.
The man is very famous.
乔遇到了一个人。这个人非常有名。

Joe met _a man_. 主句
_The man_ is very famous. 从句
=> Joe **met** _a man_ that is very famous.
乔**遇到了**一个非常有名的人。

Joe met _a man_. 从句
_The man_ is very famous. 主句
=> _The man_ that Joe met **is** very famous.
乔遇到的这个人**是**非常有名的。
```

```markdown
The subject is science.
This subject was the hardest.
这个学科是科学。这个学科是最难的。

_The subject_ is science. 主句
_This subject_ was the hardest. 从句
=> _The subject_ that was the hardest **is** science.
这个最难的学科**是**科学。
```

```markdown
They are meeting the teacher.
Everyone likes the teacher.
他们在和那个老师见面，每个人都会喜欢那个老师。

They are meeting _the teacher_. 主句
Everyone likes _the teacher_. 从句
=> They **are meeting** _the teacher_ that everyone likes.
他们在和那个每个人都喜欢的老师**见面**。
```

```markdown
The girl is a new student.
I met her in the hallway.
那个女孩是一个新学生。我在走廊里遇到了他。

_The girl_ is a new student. 主句
I met _her_ in the hallway. 从句
=> _The girl_ that I met in the hallway **is** a new student.
那个我在走廊里遇到的女孩**是**一个学生。
```

# 定语从句的引导词

## 1、引导词在从句中作主语

| 引导词               | 用法   | 例句                                                                                             |
| :------------------- | :----- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| who 或 that          | 对应人 | Middle-aged people ==who== exercise **tend to** have fewer severe health problems later in life. |
| which 或 that        | 对应物 | More consumers **are buying** vehicles ==that== use electricity.                                 |
| 有逗号时只能用 who   | 对应人 | Nelson Mandela, ==who== died in 2013, **was** president of South Africa from 1994 to 1999.       |
| 有逗号时只能用 which | 对应物 | The Nissan Leaf, ==which== was introduced in 2010, **is** an all-electric car.                   |

- https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1MY4y1P7Jj?t=3836.6&p=13
- 基本可以无脑 that，但存在特殊情况（逗号断开）
- 有逗号断开，看得更清楚更友好

- https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1MY4y1P7Jj?t=4083.5&p=13
- 一共只有四个引导词，whom 基本是个废物

## 2、引导词在从句中作宾语（可以省略）

| 引导词        | 用法   | 例句                                                                                |
| :------------ | :----- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| whom 或 that  | 对应人 | This **is** the man ==whom== you met in the school building yesterday.              |
| which 或 that | 对应物 | Scientists **are developing** a vitamin ==that== people will take only once a week. |

- https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1MY4y1P7Jj?t=4117.9&p=13

## 3、引导词在从句中作介词的宾语

| 引导词      | 用法   | 例句                                                                         |
| :---------- | :----- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 介词 +whom  | 对应人 | You **should make** eye contact with the person ==to whom== you are talking. |
| 介词 +which | 对应物 | They **conducted** a study ==in which== they surveyed young coffee drinkers. |

## 定语从句引导词的简单原则

|        | 人       | 物         |
| ------ | -------- | ---------- |
| 无逗号 | that/who | that/which |
| 有逗号 | who      | which      |
| 介词   | +whom    | +which     |

# 定语从句的基本作用

## 1、合并短句

```markdown
_Redwood trees_ **grow** mainly on the Pacific coast from Oregon to California.
_Redwood trees_ **are** the tallest trees in the world.
=> _Redwood trees_, ==which== are the tallest trees in the world, **grow** mainly on the Pacific coast from Oregon to California.
```

## 2、描述重要信息（没有用逗号断开）

```markdown
The bark of the redwood tree **contains** _chemicals_ ==that== protect the tree from insects. 没有逗号 紧紧挨着 描述重要信息
```

## 3、补充其它信息（可以用逗号断开）

```markdown
_The coast redwood tree_, ==which== is the same height as a 37-story building, **is** the tallest type of redwood tree. 逗号断开 补充信息
```

- “限定/非限定”，知道这个语法名词一点用都没有
- 限定，没有逗号，描述重要信息
- 非限定，有逗号，补充其他信息（非限定就是不重要）

## 练习：理解定语从句信息的重要性差异

```markdown

Linguists are working with native people that have languages that are disappearing.
Professor Deke, who is one of the authors of our history textbook, makes the subject interesting and important by connecting the past with the present and the future.
```

_Chlorophyll_,

==who== had a wife from a slave-owning family, 补充其它信息
