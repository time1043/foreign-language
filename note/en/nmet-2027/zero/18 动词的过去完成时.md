# 什么是过去完成时 What is Past Perfect?

- 过去完成时用于强调在过去完成动作或过去时间里之前已经结束的动作

```markdown
By nine o'clock last night, we had got 200 pictures from the spaceship.
I thought I had sent the letter a week before.
When I woke up, the rain had already stopped.
```

# 过去完成时的构成

- 助动词 had + 过去分词（had + done）

| 主语             | 结构             | 例句                                           |
| :--------------- | :--------------- | :--------------------------------------------- |
| I / You / We / They | had + done    | By the time the company closed, it had served thousands of consumers. |
| He / She / It    | had not + done   |                                              |

# 过去完成时的用法

## 1、明确两个过去结束的动作或两个过去时间里最早的动作

- 注意：需要两个过去动作和需要分出先后顺序，以上两个条件缺一不可

```markdown
By the end of last term, we had learned 5000 new words.
By the time of last year, we had built two bridges.
The bus had already left by the time I got there.
```

**常见时间词**

```
by+过去时间 / by the time+过去时间 截止到过去某时为止（可以是短语或从句）
```

**情感/惊讶/懊恼/兴奋**

```markdown
He said he had worked in that factory since 1949. 英文里语气态随主句变化
They had wanted to help but could not get there in time.
We had hoped to be able to come and see you, but we were busy.
```

**动作必须排序**

```markdown
I didn't go to the cinema because I had seen the film.
When they broke into the house, the thief had run away.
The patient entered the hospital at 10:00 p.m. with stomach pains. She had eaten shellfish.
```

## 注意：如果动作时间明确，或出现 before 或 after 等描述动作完后的时间状语从句，不需要使用过去完成时，使用一般过去即可

```markdown
I took out the key, opened the door, and entered this empty room.
I returned the book that I had borrowed/borrowed.
She found the key that she had lost/lost.
Before Germany invaded Spain, Picasso was not very interested in painting about the war.
After he arrived in England, Marx worked hard to improve his English.
```

## 2、描述过去的虚假的条件（不作为考点，看懂即可）

```markdown
If I had known your telephone number, I would have called you.
```

## 练习：判断一般过去或过去完成时

```markdown
1. By the time John Wilkes Booth shot Abraham Lincoln, the Civil War had ended with General Lee's surrender five days earlier.
2. Nelson Mandela had spent/spent 26 years in prison when he walked out in 1990.
3. On March 11, 2011, a terrible earthquake hit Japan. Few scientists had predicted such a strong earthquake and its resulting tsunami, so the country was unprepared on that day. The tsunami damaged a nuclear reactor. About 230,000 people lost their homes.
4. By the early part of the 20th century, many African-Americans had moved from the rural south to northern cities. In the South, they made their living primarily on farms until insects caused severe crop damage. They went to cities like Chicago to find jobs.
5. At the beginning of the Harry Potter series of books by J. K. Rowling, the hero, Harry Potter, lived with his aunt and uncle. His parents died when he was a baby. Fortunately, a letter arrived one day. It informed him of his acceptance to Hogwarts, which changed his future forever.
```

# 重要考点：过去时态的正确应用

- **一般过去**：陈述过去结束的事件
- **过去进行**：描述过去某个特定时间正在发生的动作
- **过去完成**：描述某个过去被突然中断的动作，描述故事背景或生动形象的场景；明确两个过去结束的动作里最早的动作

## 练习：使用正确的过去时态填空

```markdown
1. The worst wildfire in California history occurred in the San Diego area in 2003. One year after the devastating fire, however, biologists found encouraging signs that the burned area began / had begun to recover.
2. Diana, Princess of Wales, died in a car crash on August 31, 1997. At the time, she was not wearing a seatbelt.
3. According to the case study notes, the patient had experienced / experienced shortness of breath for several months before she finally made an appointment to see a doctor.
4. Automation was relatively late to enter the Japanese banking system. For example, in the 1970s, when most American banks had already used computers, banks in Japan were still using the abacus to make calculations.
5. In the tsunami that hit Indonesia and Thailand in 2004, the death number was very high because people were going about their daily lives, unaware of the upcoming danger.
6. Taxicabs in New York City must have a license, called a medallion. In 2013, the medallions cost as much as $1.3 million each.
7. The purpose of this research project was to study eye movements of young readers as they encountered easy and difficult material in a passage. As a child was reading, a computer program recorded her eye movements.
8. A 2015 study indicated that, due to the high price of printed textbooks, e-book use among college students had increased / increased rapidly in just a few years.
```

# 时态总结：七种时态的作用

## 1、只与现在有关的两种时态

**一般现在**：表示习惯、常规和日常情况（发生许多次的动作、一直都这样的动作）

**现在进行**：描述某个动作这一刻或这一段正在发生；表示现在的情况正在发生变化；强调动作反复发生，有抱怨或责备的语气

## 2、只与过去有关的三种时态

**一般过去**：陈述过去结束的事件

**过去进行**：描述过去某个特定时间正在发生的动作；描述某个过去被突然中断的动作；描述故事背景或生动形象的场景

**过去完成**：明确两个过去结束的动作里最早的动作

## 3、与过去和现在有关的一种时态

**现在完成**：表示动作在过去和现在的时间关系

## 4、与将来有关的一种时态

**一般将来**：描述将来的预测和预期、计划和提议
