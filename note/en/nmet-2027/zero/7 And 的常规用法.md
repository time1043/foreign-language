# 什么是连词 What is a Conjunction?

- 连词是**发挥连接作用**的词

# And 的基本用法：连接

- And 用于连接一个词/短语和另一个词/短语
- 另一个词/短语必须具备**相似的信息**

| 内容         | 两个句子                                                                       | 使用 and 连接                                         |
| :----------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------- |
| 名词（主语） | _My sister_ **plays** soccer. <br>_I_ **plays** soccer.                        | _My sister_ ==and== _I_ **play** soccer.              |
| 名词（宾语） | Pinar **speaks** _Korean_. <br>Pinar **speaks** _English_.                     | Pinar **speaks** _Korean_ ==and== _English_.          |
| 动词         | A teacher **plans** lessons. <br>A teacher **teaches** lessons.                | A teacher **plans** ==and== **teaches** lessons.      |
|              | I **go swimming** on vacation. <br>I **read books** on vacation.               | I **go swimming** ==and== **read books** on vacation. |
| 形容词       | The flag of Canada **is** _red_. <br>The flag of Canada **is** _white_.        | The flag of Canada **is** _red_ ==and== _white_.      |
|              | We **have** _cold_ weather in winter. <br>We **have** _wet_ weather in winter. | We **have** _cold_ ==and== _wet_ weather in winter.   |

# And 的进阶用法：列举

- 使用 and 可以连接三个或以上的内容
- 每个内容后面需要用逗号断开，最后一个除外
- 最后一个内容之前用 and 连接
- **如果并列的是短语，建议 and 之前的逗号不要省略**

| 三个句子                                                                                           | 使用 and 连接                                               |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------- |
| The flag of Mexico is _green_. <br>The flag of Mexico is _white_. <br>The flag of Mexico is _red_. | The flag of Mexico **is** _green_, _white_, ==and== _red_.  |
| _Thailand_ is in Asia. <br>_Vietnam_ is in Asia. <br>_Singapore_ is in Asia.                       | _Thailand_, _Vietnam_, ==and== _Singapore_ **are** in Asia. |

```markdown
A, B, C, and D ✅
A, B, C and D ✅ 可以省略 没有歧义

A_A, B_B, C_C, and D_D ✅
A_A, B_B, C_C and D_D 🙅 不要省略 省略后引起歧义
```

```markdown
teachers and parents
boys and girls
cats and dogs

teachers and parents, boys and girls and cats and dogs 🙅 混乱
teachers and parents, boys and girls, and cats and dogs ✅

price of the goods
location of the shop
style of fashion

price of the goods, location of the shop and style of fashion 🙅 混乱
price of the goods, location of the shop, and style of fashion ✅
```

## 常见错误

```markdown
Miami, and Orlando are in Florida.
The salad has lettuce tomatoes olives and green onions.
A boy and a girl is waiting for you.
English and Chinese are useful subject.
```

```markdown
Miami ==and== Orlando **are** in Florida. 两者and不需要逗号
The salad **has** lettuce, tomatoes, olives, ==and== green onions. 三者以上列举需要逗号
A boy ==and== a girl **are** waiting for you. 动词和主语保持一致
English ==and== Chinese **are** useful subjects. 使用and注意名词单复数变化
```

# And 连接句子：延伸句子内容（考点）

- 注意：在高考语法填空里，尽量不要用 and 连接两个句子

```markdown
// 祈使句 and 将来时句子（照做了后会出现的结果）
Work hard ==and== you will live happily. 建议→结果
Come early ==and== you will see him. 建议→结果

// 摄像机运镜
It's a fine day today, ==and== everyone is busy. 背景→具体
The car stopped, ==and== it was quickly surrounded by the police. 连续动作

// 递进（那样了还不够还要...）
If you want to be thinner and healthier, you have to eat less food – ==and== you _also_ have to take more exercise. 并列逻辑 递进
Don't be late, ==and== put on your old clothes. 并列逻辑 递进
I mean you eat too much, ==and== you don't take enough exercise. 并列逻辑 递进
```
