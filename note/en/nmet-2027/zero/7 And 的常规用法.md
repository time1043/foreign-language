# 什么是连词 What is a Conjunction?

- 连词是发挥连接作用的词

# And 的基本用法：连接

- And 用于连接一个词组和另一个词组，另一个词组必须具备相似的信息

| 内容         | 三个句子                                                                                                               | 使用 and 连接                                                                |
| :----------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 名词（主语） | My sister plays soccer. She plays soccer.                                                                              | My sister and I play soccer.                                                 |
| 名词（宾语） | Pinor speaks Korean. Pinor speaks English.                                                                             | Pinor speaks Korean and English.                                             |
| 动词         | A teacher learns lessons. A teacher teaches lessons. I go swimming on vacation. I go hiking on vacation.               | A teacher learns and teaches lessons. I go swimming and hiking on vacation.  |
| 形容词       | The flag of Canada is red. The flag of Canada is white. We have cold weather in winter. We have wet weather in winter. | The flag of Canada is red and white. We have cold and wet weather in winter. |

```markdown

5. My sister is smart and hard-working. 形容词

7. My younger brother and his friends play video games every day. 主语
8. My aunt and I like to watch old movies. 主语

```

# And 的进阶用法：列举

- 使用 and 可以连接三个或以上的内容
- 每个内容后面需要用逗号断开，最后一个除外
- 最后一个内容之前用 and 连接
- **如果并列的是短语，建议 and 之前的逗号不要省略**

| 三个句子                                                                             | 使用 and 连接                                 |
| :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------- |
| The flag of Mexico is green. The flag of Mexico is white. The flag of Mexico is red. | The flag of Mexico is green, white, and red.  |
| Thailand is in Asia. Vietnam is in Asia. Singapore is in Asia.                       | Thailand, Vietnam, and Singapore are in Asia. |

## 注意：常见错误

**两者 and 不需要逗号**

```markdown
Miami, and Orlando are in Florida.
→ Miami and Orlando are in Florida.
```

**三者以上列举需要逗号**

```markdown
The salad has lettuce tomatoes olives and green onions.
→ The salad has lettuce, tomatoes, olives, and green onions.
```

**动词和主语保持一致**

```markdown
A boy and a girl is waiting for you.
→ A boy and a girl are waiting for you.
```

**使用 and 注意名词单复数变化**

```markdown
English and Chinese are useful subject.
→ English and Chinese are useful subjects.
```

## 练习：使用 and 连接主语

```markdown
1. France is in Europe. England is in Europe. Spain is in Europe.
   France, England, and Spain are in Europe.
2. Football is a sport. Basketball is a sport. Volleyball is a sport.
   Football, basketball, and volleyball are sports.
3. Apples are delicious. Bananas are delicious. Oranges are delicious.
   Apples, bananas, and oranges are delicious.
4. Go is a simple verb. Eat is a simple verb. Take is a simple verb.
   Go, eat, and take are simple verbs.
5. Yellow is a pretty color. Green is a pretty color. Blue is a pretty color.
   Yellow, green, and blue are pretty colors.
6. Isabella is a high school student. Sophia is a high school student. David is a high school student.
   Isabella, Sophia, and David are high school students.
7. London is a city in England. Manchester is a city in England. Liverpool is a city in England.
   London, Manchester, and Liverpool are cities in England.
```

## 练习：使用 and 连接宾语

```markdown
1. Lee speaks Chinese. Lee speaks Korean. Lee speaks English.
   Lee speaks Chinese, Korean, and English.
2. I eat scrambled eggs for breakfast. I eat toast for breakfast. I eat fruit for breakfast.
   I eat scrambled eggs, toast, and fruit for breakfast.
3. My children play football. My children play video games.
   My children play football and video games.
4. Susan has a cat. Susan has five goldfish. Susan has a parrot.
   Susan has a cat, five goldfish, and a parrot.
5. My brother cooks pasta well. My brother cooks steak well.
   My brother cooks pasta and steak well.
6. Joy likes math. Joy likes science. Joy likes English. Joy likes history.
   Joy likes math, science, English, and history.
```

## 练习：使用 and 连接动词

```markdown
1. Frank lives in New York. Frank works in New York.
   Frank lives and works in New York.
2. Ducks swim. Ducks fly.
   Ducks swim and fly.
3. Maria buys the food. Maria cooks the food.
   Maria buys and cooks food.
4. Erica listens to music on the train. Erica reads the newspaper on the train.
   Erica listens to music and reads the newspaper on the train.
5. I wake up before 7 a.m. I take a shower before 7 a.m. I get dressed before 7 a.m.
   I wake up, take a shower, and get dressed before 7 a.m.
6. Students in this class read several books. Students in this class write two long reports.
   Students in this class read several books and write two long reports.
7. The professor teaches. The professor has office hours. The professor reads student papers. The professor gives grades.
   The professor teaches, has office hours, reads student papers, and gives grades.
8. Norah writes new English words in her notebook. Norah says them five times. Norah thinks of an example sentence for each one.
   Norah writes new English words in her notebook, says them five times, and thinks of an example sentence for each one.
```

# And 连接句子：延伸句子内容（高考考点）

```markdown
Work hard and you will live happily. 建议→结果
Come early and you will see him. 建议→结果

It's a fine day today, and everyone is busy. 背景→具体
The car stopped, and it was quickly surrounded by the police. 连续动作

If you want to be thinner and healthier, you have to eat less food – and you also have to take more exercise. 并列逻辑 递进
Don't be late, and put on your old clothes. 并列逻辑 递进
I mean you eat too much, and you don't take enough exercise. 并列逻辑 递进
```

**注意：在高考语法填空里，尽量不要用 and 连接两个句子**
