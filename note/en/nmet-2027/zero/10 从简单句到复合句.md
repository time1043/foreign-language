# 什么是简单句 What is a simple sentence?

- 英语句子的基本要求：一组词，有完整意思，有主语，有动词，大写字母开头
- 句号问号叹号结尾
- 简单句就是有一套 **"主语+动词结构"** 的句子
- 主语可以不止一个，动词也可以不止一个
- 但是 **"主语+动词结构" 只能有一套**

| 简单句类型           | 主语               | 动词和其它信息                |
| :------------------- | :----------------- | :---------------------------- |
| 1 个主语+1 个动词    | Japan              | imports oil.                  |
| 1 个主语+2 个动词    | Japan              | imports oil and exports vehicles. |
| 2 个主语+1 个动词    | Japan and China    | import oil.                   |
| 2 个主语+2 个动词    | Japan and China    | import oil and export vehicles. |

# 简单句的格式

## 1、陈述

| 简单句类型             | 例句                       |
| :--------------------- | :------------------------- |
| 主语+动词（+宾语）     | An earthquake hit Sichuan. |
| 介词短语+主语+动词     | In 2008, an earthquake hit Sichuan. |
| 主语+be 动词+形容词    | German and English are similar. |
| 主语+be 动词+介词短语  | Germany is in Europe.      |

## 2、命令和请求（祈使句）

| 简单句类型                 | 例句                           |
| :------------------------- | :----------------------------- |
| 动词原形（+宾语+介词短语） | Consider the cost of travel.   |
| 主语 you 被省略了           | Please make your decision carefully. |

## 3、疑问

| 简单句类型                 | 例句                           |
| :------------------------- | :----------------------------- |
| 助动词+主语+动词原形       | Do you run in the morning?     |
| 疑问词+助动词+主语+动词原形 | How do you choose the running route? |
| be 动词+主语+形容词        | Is the running necessary?      |
| 疑问词+be 动词+主语        | What are the benefits?         |

**助动词：do, does, did, am, is, are, was, were, has, have, had, can, will**

# 简单句的功能

## 1、陈述信息——简单句最常见的应用

```markdown
Barack Obama became president in 2009.
This paper will discuss the economic situation in Argentina.
```

## 2、发出命令、提出请求——用于指挥读者的行为

```markdown
Note the difference between these two types of animals.
Let us now turn our attention to the third category.
```

## 3、提出问题——引起读者关注，引发读者思考

```markdown
How does aspirin work? This question has puzzled scientists for centuries. 引出文章主题
Will this new plan really solve the problem? You will not be disappointed. 引出作者态度
```

## 练习：分辨三种简单句的功能

```markdown
1. Of the world's 17 types of penguins, the largest penguins live in very cold climates. 陈述
2. What factors impact the migration of manatees? 提问
3. Note the increase in oxygen flow in the next step of this experiment. 命令请求
4. For a variety of health reasons, the government should pass laws about smoking in public places. 陈述
5. At this point, let us consider the differences between face-to-face and online classes. 命令请求
6. Without a doubt, physical exercise can greatly improve the health of young children. 陈述
7. Imagine living in a world without the Internet. 命令请求
8. What roles will robots play in our future? 疑问
```

# 什么是复合句 What is a compound sentence?

- 使用基本连词把两个简单句连在一起变成更长的句子，称为复合句
- 复合句有两套或更多的 **"主语+动词结构"**
- 复合句连词 FANBOYS：for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so
- 复合句中两个简单句简单句对等

| 简单句 1              | 基本连词 | 简单句 2                |
| :-------------------- | :------- | :---------------------- |
| Japan imports oil     | and      | China imports gas.      |
| Japan imports oil     | but      | Saudi Arabia exports it.|
| Japan imports oil     | so       | oil price in Japan is high. |

**注意：基本连词前面需要用逗号断开，不要用基本连词连接两个独立句**

```markdown
错误 Japan imports oil and China imports gas.
正确 Japan imports oil, and China imports gas.

错误 Japan imports oil. But Saudi Arabia exports it.
正确 Japan imports oil, but Saudi Arabia exports it.
```

# 基本连词的作用

- **and** 补充额外信息 extra information
- **but** 补充相反或对立的信息 opposite information
- **so** 表示结果或目的 result / purpose

## 注意：常见错误

**基本连词前面无逗号**

```markdown
Five is an odd number, but six is an even number.
```

**基本连词使用错误**

```markdown
I like tea, but I drink coffee sometimes.
```

**基本连词不连接两个独立句**

```markdown
Costa Rica is in Central America, and Peru is in South America.
两个句子不能逗号连接
July is usually a month with a great deal of rainfall, but this year it has been very dry.
```

## 练习：分辨简单句和复合句

```markdown
1. The tiny nation of Liechtenstein lies in central Europe and has no seacoast. 简单
2. At many universities, a major in history requires 36 credits of coursework, but a minor requires only 18 credits. 复合
3. According to several world sources, the five most common surnames in England are Smith, Jones, Williams, Taylor, and Brown. 简单
4. Machu Picchu is a very popular destination, so Peru receives millions of tourists each year. 复合
5. The word present has two pronunciations, but the meanings are very different. 复合
6. Giraffes can eat up to 100 pounds of leaves per day, so they spend most of their day eating. 复合
7. Singapore has been an independent country for more than five decades, but it was part of Malaysia. 复合
```

# 基本连词的具体用法

## 1、and 的用法

- 两分句逗号断开，两词不需要逗号

```markdown
China is in Asia, and it has a large population.
China is in Asia and has a large population.
China and Japan are in Asia.
China, Korea, and Japan are in Asia.
```

## 2、but 的用法

- 两分句逗号断开，两词不需要逗号

```markdown
Shanghai is a large city, but it is not China's capital.
The weather in January is sunny but cold.
I feel nervous but excited.
A 和 B 一致 and  A 和 B 相反 but
```

## 3、so 的用法

- 表示结果，所以：需要逗号断开
- 表示目的，为了这样才能：不需要逗号断开

```markdown
I study hard, so I have a good grade in the exam.
I study hard so that I can have a bright future.
```

```markdown

   A giraffe has four long legs, so it can run really fast.
3. Each shirt is $50. Two shirts are $100.
   Each shirt is $50, so two shirts are $100.
4. The word on has more than 20 meanings. It is difficult to learn well.
   The word on has more than 20 meanings, so it is difficult to learn well.
5. The weather in Florida is hot in July. That is not a good time to visit there.
   The weather in Florida is hot in July, so that is not a good time to visit there.
6. Marco's family is from England. But he does not speak English.
   Marco's family is from England, but he does not speak English.
7. I have a desktop computer. I never use it.
   I have a desktop computer, but I never use it.
8. You like milk in your coffee. I like my coffee black.
   You like milk in your coffee, but I like my coffee black.
9. Wei is from China. He lives in the United States.
   Wei is from China, and he lives in the United States.
```
