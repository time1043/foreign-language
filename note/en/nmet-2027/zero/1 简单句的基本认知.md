# 什么是句子 What is a Sentence?

1. 一组词 a group of words
2. 有完整含义 complete idea
3. 有主语 person or thing + does or is something
4. 有动词 what the subject does or is

- one apple and one banana 是短语，不是句子
- I understand English. ✅
- My friend reads quickly. ✅
- That cat is black. ✅

- 结论：**主语**往往在句子的开头部分，**动词**往往跟在主语的后面

## 注意

- 所有句子都要以大写字母开始
- 陈述句以句号 "." 结尾；疑问句以问号 "?" 结尾；感叹句以叹号 "!" 结尾
- 在英语的句子中，严禁**逗号**连接两个句子

## 练习：找出错误

| 错误                         | 原因     | 修正                             |
| :--------------------------- | :------- | :------------------------------- |
| That cat is black and white  | 缺句号   | That cat **is** black and white. |
| that cat is black and white. | 没大写   | That cat **is** black and white. |
| Is black and white.          | 没主语   | XXX **is** black and white.      |
| That cat black and white.    | 没动词   | That cat **is** black and white. |
| Is black and white that cat. | 顺序混乱 | That cat **is** black and white. |

## 练习：找出主语和动词

- My last name **has** five letters.
- My last name **is** Adams.
- My first name **is** Ann.
- My first name **has** three letters.
- I **come** from Canada.
- My family and I **live** in Vancouver.
- I **like** watermelons, apples, bananas, and oranges.
- A watermelon **is** green and red.
- Lemons **are** yellow.
- My favorite food **is** salad.

I **come** from Canada. ✅ 介词为名词服务

## 练习：组合句子

```
this a bird is animal
a is parrot it
this colors five has parrot
the red head and is white
bird this beautiful is very
it smart very is
can parrot this talk
```

```markdown
This animal **is** a bird. // 名词前搭配冠词
It **is** a parrot.
This parrot **has** five colors.
The head **is** red and white.
This bird **is** very beautiful.
It **is** very smart.
This parrot **can talk**. // can 情态动词
```

- 句子的首先是确定**动词**
