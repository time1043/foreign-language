# 什么是谓语 What is Predicate?

- 谓语描述句子主语的动作或状态特征
- 谓语指明主语 "做什么"、"是什么" 或 "是什么样的"
- 谓语可以是单独动词的时态变化，也可以是动词加形容词，可以是情态动词加动词
- 谓语有主动和被动两种不同的形态

```markdown
I made your birthday cake last night.
Your birthday cake was made by me last night.
This book is my gift for you.
I felt tired after a day's work. / My pen is in my bag.
I can finish the task on my own.
This city was built in Tang Dynasty to defend people.
```

# 情态动词 共 12 个

| 情态动词肯定   | 情态动词否定     | 例句                                           |
| :------------- | :--------------- | :--------------------------------------------- |
| can / could + do | can / could not + do | A tiger can swim nearly six kilometres. In high school, Jesse Owens could run 100 yards in 9.4 seconds. |
| will / would + do | will / would not + do | I will cherish our friendship forever. It would be a mistake to disregard this fact. |
| may / might + do | may / might not + do | Someday, people may live in man-made cities that float on the ocean. You might make a mistake. |
| must + do      | must not + do    | We all know that these workers must wash their hands. |
| should + do    | should not + do  | Parents should read to their children. |
| have to + do   | do not have to + do | Children have to attend school. |
| be able to + do | be not able to + do | Ducks are able to fly up to 60 miles an hour. |
| be supposed to + do | be not supposed to + do | You are supposed to be here. |
| ought to + do  | —                | We ought to respect the elders. |

# 情态动词的作用

| 作用                     | 情态动词                     | 例句                                           |
| :----------------------- | :--------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------- |
| 表示现在或过去的能力     | can, could, be able to       | Some animals can predict earthquakes up to five days before one happens. He said he would be able to finish the work in an hour, but he has not. |
| 给出意见、建议或评论     | should, must, ought to       | In sum, 16-year-olds should be able to vote in national elections. Students ought to obey the rules. |
| 表示推测、不确定或怀疑的语气 | may, might, can, could   | Some people believe that urban farming could solve many of the world's hunger problems. |
| 强调必要性或要求         | must, have to, ought to      | The dust storms last year were so bad that everyone had to wear a mask outside. The researchers must not reveal the results of the experiment. |
| 表示意愿或将来的计划     | will                         | I will take the challenge. This chart will compare daily life in Tokyo with daily life in Beijing. |

## 练习：判断情态动词的作用

```markdown

3. Until the Berlin Wall came down in 1989, East Germans could not travel freely. 过去的能力

```

# 被动语态

- 在被动语态的语句中，**动作的宾语处于主语的位置**

```markdown
主动 The doctor examined the child.
被动 The child was examined (by the doctor).
```

## 重要考点：被动语态的变化

- 动词变为 be 动词+过去分词（do 变为 be+done）

| 主语       | 肯定             | 例句                                           |
| :--------- | :--------------- | :--------------------------------------------- |
| do / does 一般现在 | am/is/are + done | New cars are tested before they are delivered to the market. New cars are not delivered to the market before they are tested. |
| did 一般过去 | was/were + done  | A new species of wild tomato was discovered recently. Wild tomatoes were not grown in England until the late 16th century. |
| will do 一般将来 | will be + done | The job will be finished today. The book will not be returned tomorrow. |
|            | will not be + done |                                              |

| 动词          | 结构                | 例句                                           |
| :------------ | :------------------ | :--------------------------------------------- |
| have/has 现在完成 | have/has been + done | Drones have been used by the American military since 1915. Although many earthquakes has been predicted, their exact locations have not been found. |
| had done 过去完成 | had been + done     | When they came to the scene, the window had been broken. |
| am/is/are doing 现在进行 | am/is/are being + done | — |
| was/were doing 过去进行 | was/were being + done | — |
| 情态动词 + do | 情态动词 + be + done | Many coastal cities may be affected by rising seas in the future. In hot weather, pets should not be left in cars. |

## 注意：

1、**动作有宾语，才能变成被动语态**
2、**注意 be 动词或助动词和主语保持一致**
3、**如果不需要强调动作发送者，"by 短语" 可以省略**
4、**进行时态被动比较罕见，可以省略**
5、**动词过去分词的变化必须掌握**

# 什么是非谓语 What is Non-Predicate?

- 在句子不是谓语的动词，都是非谓语动词
- 谓语体现动作时间，非谓语与时间没有关系
- 谓语受人称和单复数限制，非谓语完全不受影响
- **非谓语动词也要变为名词作用，非谓语动词没有主被动**

```markdown
Last week, Miss Mary came to our classroom.
Last week, Miss Mary came to our classroom to have a talk with us.
Larry likes the pop music.
Larry has nothing to do today.
```

# 重要考点：非谓语动词的基本形态

| 种类     | 形式   | 作用                                     |
| :------- | :----- | :--------------------------------------- |
| 动名词   | doing  | 句子主语、动词宾语、介词宾语             |
| 不定式   | to do  | 动词宾语、补充宾语、补充主语、目的状语   |
| 现在分词 | doing  | 形容词、副词                             |
| 过去分词 | done   | 形容词、副词                             |

## 注意：

**1、非谓语动词的否定直接在前面加 not**

**2、动名词是单数名词**

**3、不定式 to do 一般不用作主语，也不用作介词宾语**

## 练习：辨认句子中的非谓语动词

```markdown
1. Exercising just 30 minutes a day can lead to good heart health.
   exercising 主语 动名词
2. Many students avoid making outlines before they begin to write.
   making 宾语 动名词 / to write 宾语 不定式
3. Education majors look forward to helping students succeed.
   helping 宾语 动名词
4. The company's marketing strategy failed to attract new customers.
   marketing 形容词 现在分词 / to attract 宾语 不定式
5. It is possible to receive on-the-job training for some careers.
   to receive 补充形容词 不定式 / training 宾语 动名词
6. Not taking time to relax lowers one's stress level.
   not taking 主语 动名词 / to relax 补充名词 不定式
7. Many students worry about not finding a job after they graduate.
   not finding 宾语 动名词
8. To address global warming, it is important not to waste water, food, or energy.
   to address 目的 不定式 / not to waste 补充形容词 不定式
9. Though the crime was seen by many people, there were no known suspects.
   known 形容词 过去分词
10. It was an interesting problem, with no clear solution.
    interesting 形容词 现在分词
11. Astronaut Chris Hadfield's photos of Earth are astonishing in their color and detail.
    astonishing 形容词 现在分词
12. At the end of the course, 90 percent of the students said they felt satisfied.
    satisfied 形容词 过去分词
```
