# 现在分词和过去分词作形容词的用法

| 常规用法 |         |                                    |
| :------- | :------ | :--------------------------------- |
| doing    | 主动、进行中 | running water (water that is running) / aching leg (leg that is aching) |
| done     | 被动、已完成 | targeted advertising (advertising that is targeted) / burned house (house that is burned) |

| 描述情绪感受 |         |                                    |
| :----------- | :------ | :--------------------------------- |
| doing        | 描述事物人物特点 | Shakespeare wrote entertaining plays. The news was depressing. |
| done         | 描述人物心理感受 | The audience was excited. Teenagers should be cared more. |

# 形容词从句与分词的转换规则

## 1、出现 be 动词的形容词从句，省略引导词和 be 动词

```markdown
The fish that is disturbing the local ecosystem is known as the snakehead fish.
The fish disturbing the local ecosystem is known as the snakehead fish.

The lecture hall that was built in 2005 belongs to my high school.
The lecture hall built in 2005 belongs to my high school.
```

## 2、常规动词的定语从句，省略引导词，动词变成 doing

```markdown
Anyone who hopes to be a pilot must have at least 250 hours of flight time.
Anyone hoping to be a pilot must have at least 250 hours of flight time.
```

## 练习：判断形容词从句中的动词，然后转换为分词

```markdown
1. Countries that (lies / lie) near the equator (has / have) extremely high temperatures.
   Countries lying near the equator have extremely high temperatures.
2. The person who (was / were) honored at the ceremony started one of the biggest companies in Silicon Valley.
   The person honored at the ceremony started one of the biggest companies in Silicon Valley.
3. Cars that (runs / run) on electricity (saves / save) gas and are highly praised by environmentalists.
   Cars running on electricity save gas and are highly praised by environmentalists.
4. Researchers who (is / are) trying to find a cure for cancer sometimes (works / work) at large universities.
   Researchers trying to find a cure for cancer sometimes work at large universities.
5. The Nobel Prizes that (is / are) awarded each year (is / are) for physics, chemistry, medicine, and literature.
   The Nobel Prizes awarded each year are for physics, chemistry, medicine, and literature.
6. The number of women who (goes / go) to school in Afghanistan (has / have) increased by 10 percent.
   The number of women going to school in Afghanistan has increased by 10 percent.
```

# 副词从句与分词的转换规则

## 1、前提条件：主句与副词从句主语相同

- 可转换 When I saw his face, I recalled his name.
- 不可转换 When he greeted me, I replied warmly.

## 2、出现 be 动词的副词从句，省略引导词和主语和 be 动词

- 描述同时的时间副词从句，引导词可以一并省略

```markdown
When people are stressed, they exhibit high blood pressure and accelerated heart rates.
When stressed, people exhibit high blood pressure and accelerated heart rates.
Stressed, people exhibit high blood pressure and accelerated heart rates.

When I am listening to the sound of the waves, I feel peaceful.
When listening to the sound of the waves, I feel peaceful.
Listening to the sound of the waves, I feel peaceful.

When the girl was asked about the reason for winning the prize, she owed the success to her teachers.
When asked about the reason for winning the prize, the girl owed the success to her teachers.
Asked about the reason for winning the prize, the girl owed the success to her teachers.
```

## 3、常规动词的副词从句，省略主语，动词变成 doing

- 描述同时的时间副词从句，引导词可以一并省略

```markdown
When people sleep with pressure, they dream four to six times each night.
When sleeping with pressure, people dream four to six times each night.
Sleeping with pressure, people dream four to six times each night.

Although he faced many challenges, he still kept his dream in mind.
Although facing many challenges, he still kept his dream in mind.

If you don't try your best, you will not taste the happiness of success.
If not trying your best, you will not taste the happiness of success.
You will not taste the happiness of success if not trying your best.
```

## 4、描述原因的副词从句，省略引导词 because 或 since 和主语，动词变成 doing

```markdown
Because the students had read the instruction, they could carry out the experiment alone.
Having read the instruction, the students could carry out the experiment alone.
```
