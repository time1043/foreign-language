# 什么是名词性从句 What is a Noun Clause?

- 名词：人物、事物、地点，可以做主语或宾语
- 英语句子：一组词，有完整含义，有主语，有动词
- 从句：由引导词开始的句子，不能脱离主句独立存在
- 疑问词：提出问题的词，如 who, whom, whose, which, where, when, why, how, how long, how many, how much, how often 等
- **名词性从句就是一个从句作为一个名词，由 that，疑问词或 if/whether 引导**

| 名词性从句的类型       | 例句                                           |
| :--------------------- | :--------------------------------------------- |
| that + 主语 + 动词     | Scientists announce that they find other planets. |
| 疑问词 + 主语 + 动词   | What the researchers discovered changed the medical world. Many graduates do not know where they should apply for jobs after college. The book shows what happened thousands of years ago. |
| if/whether + 主语 + 动词 | People do not know if the damage to the city is serious. Whether you can pass the exam depends on your efforts. |

## 练习：找出句中的名词性从句

```markdown
1. In a recent nationwide poll, respondents stated whether they felt optimistic or pessimistic about a number of economic issues.
2. What defines a person's success varies from individual to individual.
3. Economic theory experts believe that increasing interest rates results in decreased consumer spending.
4. People commonly ask themselves if their work makes them happy.
5. Career counselors advise that who you know will help you find a job.
6. It is not surprising that so many people live to be 100 or older these days.
7. It is a fact that the media influences teens in several ways, including hair and clothing styles.
8. Many newly arrived immigrants do not know where they can find help with education and employment needs.
```

## 注意：

**1、名词性从句视作单数名词，动词需要使用单数**

```markdown
How often people change careers is an interesting question.
```

**2、that 从句作宾语时，that 可以省略**

```markdown
Research suggests (that) salty food is harmful to your health.
```

**3、注意名词性从句中主语和动词的顺序**

```markdown
错误 They are not sure who is he.
正确 They are not sure who he is.
```

## 名词性从句常见错误

**1、从句中主语和动词的顺序**

**2、从句视作单数名词，作主语的从句与动词保持一致**

## 练习：改正名词性从句中主语和动词顺序的错误

```markdown
1. A common concern among older citizens is how long will they be able to live alone.
   A common concern among older citizens is how long they will be able to live alone.
2. Doctors and nutritionists continue to debate whether are eggs good or bad for one's health.
   Doctors and nutritionists continue to debate whether eggs are good or bad for one's health.
3. There are several ways to determine if does a computer have a virus or not.
   There are several ways to determine if a computer has a virus or not.
4. Linguistic anthropologists continue to explore when did communication begin to evolve into language.
   Linguistic anthropologists continue to explore when communication began to evolve into language.
5. Whether will pollution decrease remains to be seen.
   Whether pollution will decrease remains to be seen.
```

## 练习：改正名词性从句主谓动词一致的错误

```markdown
1. The fact that today's family structures are complex bring many social changes.
   The fact that today's family structures are complex brings many social changes.
2. What makes students change their thinking depend on their learning method.
   What makes students change their thinking depends on their learning method.
3. The fact that people abandon their pets receive wide concern.
   The fact that people abandon their pets receives wide concern.
4. How students prepare for exams differ from one individual to another.
   How students prepare for exams differs from one individual to another.
```

**主语 + 动词 + 从句 找到引导词断开**
**从句 + 动词 + 宾语 找到第二个没关系的动词断开**

## 练习：填入动词的正确形式

```markdown
1. What you eat before bed often affects (affect) quality of your sleep.
2. Where sea turtles migrate is (be) difficult to determine definitively.
3. Researchers find that using a large bottle to feed babies leads (lead) to weight gain.
4. How bosses treat their employees' good judgment has (have) an impact on whether or not the employees find work meaningful.
5. What we define as high-speed Internet is (be) accessible to only 10 percent of Americans.
6. The study shows that how much fiber people eat impacts (impact) longevity.
   how much fiber people eat impacts (impact) longevity.
```

# 名词性从句的作用

## 1、提出想法和观点（应用）

```markdown
Jean Piaget, a Swiss psychologist, believe that the thinking process in children is different from that in adults.
I believe that you will like Chinese food.
```

## 2、描述发现和具体情况（理解）

```markdown
Scientists find that English learning in childhood is beneficial.
Recent studies suggest / show that generous people are generally happier than others.
```

## 3、给出建议或推荐（应用）

```markdown
Health experts recommend / suggest that adults should get between seven and eight hours of sleep each night.
I recommend / suggest that you should start from Chinese novels.
I strongly recommend that you should start from Chinese novels because the language is easy and interesting.
```

# 名词性从句的位置

## 1、作为动词的主语

```markdown
Which career a person chooses tells a lot about his or her personality.
Whether a business will succeed or fail depends on several factors.
```

## 2、作为动词的宾语

```markdown
Until 1897, doctors did not know how they could cure some disease.
A recent survey of college students shows that many do not plan to attend graduate school.
```

**常见相关动词：announce, ask, believe, claim, conclude, indicate, know, note, report, say, show, state, suggest, think 等**

## 3、作为介词的宾语（在这种情况下不使用 that）

```markdown
The workers receive training in how they can handle different situations.
He received a letter about what he should prepare for the college.
```

## 4、在 "be 动词+动词+形容词" 之后

```markdown
It is clear that many students do not completely understand English grammar.
It is true that repeat customers are important to a business.
```

**常见相关形容词：aware, certain, clear, obvious, sure, true, worried 等**

## 5、在某些名词后，给出具体内容

```markdown
He raises an idea that today's parents try to manage their children's lives.
The fact that global warming becomes serious continues to be a hot topic.
He left a message that he would come later.
```

**常见相关名词：idea, news, fact, sign, promise, question, thought, hope, message, suggestion, decision, evidence 等**

## 练习：判断名词性从句是主语还是宾语

```markdown
1. Studies suggest that low-calorie sweeteners may be just as bad as sugar for controlling weight gain. 宾语
2. Why ancient people created the Nazca lines in Peru is still not completely understood. 主语
3. Have you ever wondered why some people have an accent and others do not? 宾语
4. DNA results suggest Neanderthals and humans may share some genetic characteristics. 宾语
5. Ban Ki-moon, the Secretary-General of the United Nations, said that it is important for all us to do everything we can to secure lasting peace. 宾语
6. How infancy varies across cultures around the world is the subject of the documentary Babies. 主语
7. A thousand years ago, people believed that the earth was flat. 宾语
8. Publishers have just announced that Christopher Marlowe will be listed as a co-author on Henry VI. 宾语
9. Some scientists believe there is life on Mars. 宾语
10. What kind of exercise you do is less important than how often you do it. 主语、主语
```

# 重要考点：名词性从句中 that 与 what 的区分

- that 用于陈述句，句子内容完整
- what 用于提出问题，句子内容不完整

## 练习：区分 that 和 what 的使用

```markdown
1. Historians are not completely certain (that / what) caused the Great Depression.
2. Ancient peoples thought (that / what) the sun revolved around the earth.
3. Companies discovered (that / what) adding more sugar and salt to food products made them taste better.
4. Biologists have observed (that / what) happens to young elephants that have seen their parents killed.
5. (That / What) caused the Dancing Plague of 1518 is not known, but about 400 people in Strasbourg danced for days without stopping.
6. This paper will discuss (that / what) influences successful weight loss.
7. This research shows (that / what) poverty negatively impacts student achievement.
8. The Centers for Disease Control determined (that / what) the virus was transmitted by rats.
9. The study looked at (that / what) factors increased longevity.
10. Economists worried (that / what) the United Kingdom's vote to leave the European Union would affect global markets.
```
