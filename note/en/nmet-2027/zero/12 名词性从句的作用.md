# 什么是名词性从句 What is a Noun Clause?

- 名词：人物、事物、地点，可以做主语或宾语
- 英语句子：一组词，有完整含义，有主语，有动词
- 从句：由引导词开始的句子，不能脱离主句独立存在
- 疑问词：提出问题的词，如 who, whom, whose, which, where, when, why, how, how long, how many, how much, how often 等
- **名词性从句**就是**一个从句作为一个名词**，由 **that、疑问词或 if/whether** 引导

| 名词性从句的类型                      | 例句                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| :------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| that + 主语 + 动词                    | Scientists **announce** ==that== they find other planets.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 疑问词 + 主语 + 动词<br>疑问词 + 动词 | ==What== the researchers discovered **changed** the medical treatments. <br>Many graduates **do not know** ==where== they should apply for jobs after college. <br>The book **shows** ==what== happened thousands of years ago. |
| if/whether + 主语 + 动词<br>（是否）  | People **do not know** ==if== the damage to the city is serious. <br>==Whether== you can pass the exam **depends** on your efforts.                                                                                             |

```markdown
Many graduates **do not know** _this thing_.
Many graduates **do not know** ==where== they should apply for jobs after college.
```

## 注意

1. 名词性从句视作**单数名词**，动词需要使用单数
2. that 从句作宾语时，that 可以省略

```markdown
He **tells** me ==why== we will have a meeting.
```

## 名词性从句常见错误

1. 从句中**主语和动词的顺序**
2. 从句视作**单数名词**，作主语的从句与动词保持一致

## 练习：改正名词性从句中主语和动词顺序的错误

```markdown
A common concern among older citizens is how long will they be able to live alone.

Doctors and nutritionists continue to debate whether are eggs good or bad for one's health.

There are several ways to determine if does a computer have a virus or not.

Linguistic anthropologists continue to explore when did communication begin to evolve into language.

As the number of electric cars increases, whether will pollution decrease remains to be seen.
```

```markdown
A common concern among older citizens is how long will they be able to live alone.
A common concern _among older citizens_ **is** ==how long== they **will be able** to live alone.

Doctors and nutritionists continue to debate whether are eggs good or bad for one's health.
Doctors and nutritionists **continue** to debate ==whether== eggs **are** good or bad _for one's health_.

There are several ways to determine if does a computer have a virus or not.
There **are** several ways to determine ==if== a computer **has** a virus or not.

Linguistic anthropologists continue to explore when did communication begin to evolve into language.
Linguistic anthropologists **continue** to explore ==when== communication **began** to evolve into language.

As the number of electric cars increases, whether will pollution decrease remains to be seen.
==As== the number of electric cars **increases**, ==Whether== pollution **will decrease** **remains** to be seen.
```

## 练习：改正名词性从句主谓动词一致的错误

- `主语 + 动词 + 从句` 找到引导词断开
- `从句 + 动词 + 宾语` 找到第二个没关系的动词断开

```markdown
The fact that today's family structures are complex bring many social changes.

What makes students change their thinking depend on their learning method.

The fact that people abandon their pets receive wide concern.

How students prepare for exams differ from one individual to another.
```

```markdown
The fact that today's family structures are complex bring many social changes.
The fact ==that== today's family structures are complex **brings** many social changes.

What makes students change their thinking depend on their learning method.
==What== makes students change their thinking **depends** on their learning method.

The fact that people abandon their pets receive wide concern.
The fact ==that== people abandon their pets **receives** wide concern.

How students prepare for exams differ from one individual to another.
==How== students prepare for exams **differs** _from one individual to another_.
```


```markdown

```

## 1、作为动词的主语

```markdown
==Which== career a person chooses **tells** a lot about his or her personality.
```

- 常见相关动词：announce, ask, believe, claim, conclude, indicate, know, note, report, say, show, state, suggest, think 等


## 3、作为介词的宾语（在这种情况下不使用 that）


# 重要考点：名词性从句中 `that` 与 `what` 的区分

- that 用于陈述句，句子内容完整
- what 用于**提出问题**，句子内容不完整

## 练习：区分 `that` 和 `what` 的使用

```markdown
Historians are not completely certain (that / what) caused the Great Depression.
Ancient peoples thought (that / what) the sun revolved around the earth.
Companies discovered (that / what) adding more sugar and salt to food products made them taste better.
Biologists have observed (that / what) happens to young elephants that have seen their parents killed.
(That / What) caused the Dancing Plague of 1518 is not known, but about 400 people in Strasbourg danced for days without stopping.
This paper will discuss (that / what) influences successful weight loss.
This research shows (that / what) poverty negatively impacts student achievement.
The Centers for Disease Control determined (that / what) the virus was transmitted by rats.
The study looked at (that / what) factors increased longevity.
Economists worried (that / what) the United Kingdom's vote to leave the European Union would affect global markets.
```

```markdown
Historians **are not completely certain** ==what== _caused the Great Depression_. 从句不完整，缺主语
Ancient peoples **thought** ==that== _the sun revolved around the earth_. 从句完整
Companies **discovered** ==that== _adding more sugar and salt to food products made them taste better_. 从句完整
Biologists have **observed** ==what== _happens to young elephants_ ==that== _have seen their parents killed_. 从句不完整，缺宾语
==What== _caused the Dancing Plague of 1518_ **is** not known, but about 400 people in Strasbourg danced for days without stopping. 从句不完整，缺主语
This paper **will discuss** ==what== _influences successful weight loss_. 从句不完整，缺主语
This research **shows** ==that== _poverty negatively impacts student achievement_. 从句完整
The Centers for Disease Control **determined** ==that== _the virus was transmitted by rats_. 从句完整
The study **looked** at ==what== _factors increased longevity_. 从句不完整，缺主语的形容词；介词后面不能写that
Economists **worried** ==that== _the United Kingdom's vote to leave the European Union would affect global markets_. 从句完整
```
