# 什么是过去进行时 What is Past Progressive?

- 过去进行时表示过去正在发生的动作
- 过去：准确的过去某一刻时间，模糊的过去某一段时间

```markdown
We were watching TV from seven to nine last night.
When I saw him, he was decorating his room.
My brother fell when he was riding his bicycle.
```

# 过去进行时的构成

- be 动词+动词-ing 形式（was/were+doing）

| 主语         | be 动词   | 动词+ing 形式 | 例句                                           |
| :----------- | :-------- | :------------ | :--------------------------------------------- |
| I / He / She / It | was (not) | doing    | In 1910, Pierre Bonnard was living in Paris when he decided to move to France. |
| We / You / They | were (not) | doing   | As the actors were leaving the stage, all of the lights went out. |

## 练习：填入正确的过去进行时

```markdown
1. When she lived in Portugal in the early 1990s, J.K. Rowling was teaching English.
2. From 1909 to 1913, the American architect Frank Lloyd Wright was living in Germany.
3. In the 1960s, many African-Americans were fighting for civil rights.
4. Barack Obama was working in poor neighborhoods of Chicago during the mid-1980s.
5. During the 2008 financial crisis, it was difficult for college graduates to find jobs because many industries were not hiring new employees.
6. Beatle John Lennon was touring Britain in 1963 when his son Julian was born.
7. Barbie dolls were not selling well in China, so Mattel closed the Shanghai Barbie store.
8. When Mark Zuckerberg started Facebook, he was attending Harvard.
```

# 过去进行时的用法

## 1、描述在过去某个特定时间正在发生的动作

```markdown
The economy was showing signs of failing in 2015.
At 10:30, the crowd was gathering outside the town hall.
```

## 2、描述某个过去被突然中断的动作（重要考点）

- 使用 **while/whereas** 过去进行时描述平凡的动作过程
- 使用 **when** 一般过去时描述不平凡的突发动作

```markdown
My friends were driving through town 主句 过程
when the earthquake hit. 从句 突然发生

As they were approaching the top of Mt. Everest. 从句 过程
Bishop and Jerstad noticed that their oxygen was low. 主句 突然发生

One of the front tires blew up. 主句 突然发生
while the pilot was landing the plane. 从句 过程
```

## 3、描述故事背景或生动形象的场景

```markdown
It was an ordinary day at work. Dave was working at his desk. His colleagues were doing research on that strange equipment.
He reads a book. 他读书
He is reading a book. 他正在读书 场景感 画面感
```

## 注意：常见错误

**1、The fire was burn in the house when the firefighters arrived.**

```markdown
The fire was burning in the house when the firefighters arrived.
英文里永远不可能出现 be do 或 be did 这种情况
```

**2、J. K. Rowling teaching English in Portugal when she started the first Harry Potter book.**

```markdown
J. K. Rowling was teaching English in Portugal when she started the first Harry Potter book.
```

**3、In the early 1960s, politicians were worked to pass civil rights laws.**

```markdown
In the early 1960s, politicians were working to pass civil rights laws.
```

**4、Students were taking notes. Students were texting. Students were surfing the Internet.**

```markdown
Students were taking notes, texting, and surfing the Internet. 并列合并
```

**5、The soldiers crossed the Potomac in a storm when their boats were sinking.**

```markdown
The soldiers were crossing the Potomac in a storm when their boats sank.
```

**6、At 6:30 a.m., hundreds of voters walked in line at the election polling stations.**

```markdown
At 6:30 a.m., hundreds of voters were waiting in line at the election polling stations.
```

**7、Upon their arrival in the colonies, the English settlers were seeing Native Americans for the first time.**

```markdown
Upon their arrival in the colonies, the English settlers saw Native Americans for the first time.
```

**8、Barack Obama was elected president in 2008. He became president in January 2009, and he is president for eight years.**

```markdown
Barack Obama was elected president in 2008. He became president in January 2009, and he was president for eight years. 注意时态一致性
```

**注意：一般过去时用于陈述过去结束的事件**

```markdown
I visited Mexico in 2017.
Seventy-five students completed a survey on their use of technology for assignments.
After an unusually heavy rainfall, the river overflowed its banks and flooded the town.
The first president debate on TV occurred in 1960 between John F. Kennedy and Richard Nixon.
```
