# 什么是状语从句 What is an Adverb Clause?

- 副词：给句子增加更多信息
- 英语句子：一组词，有完整含义，有主语，有动词
- 从句：由引导词开始的句子，不能脱离主句独立存在

- **状语从句**就是一个从句作为副词，给句子增加更多信息
- 状语从句的**引导词**表示**从句和主句的关系**
- 状语从句可以放在主句前，也可以放在主句后，放在主句前需要逗号断开
- 状语从句就是**副词从句**

## 状语从句引导词表示从句和主句的六种关系

```markdown
We will have a meeting.
We will have a meeting ==when== he comes. 时间
We will have a meeting ==because== we have a problem. 原因
We will have a meeting ==so== we can solve the problem. 目的
We will have a meeting ==if== we have time. 条件
We will have a meeting ==although== we do not have time. 让步/妥协/转折
We will have a meeting ==as if== we are adults. 模样
```

```markdown
他 _高兴地_ 对我说话。
他对我说话，_好像认识我_。

他 _自信地_ 开始修理电脑。
他开始修理电脑，_好像早就知道了问题在哪_。
```

| until 直到 |                                                                                                 |

## 3、条件引导词

|                              | 状语从句                                                           |

## 练习：选择正确的状语从句引导词

```markdown
Teenagers often feel (as if / although) they are alone.
Sea levels will continue to rise (while / unless) climate change is slowed down.
(Because / Before) children do not get enough exercise, they are becoming unhealthier.
(Once / Since) unemployment has increased in the past year, consumer spending has dropped greatly.
(When / Even if) athletes train too hard, they often get injuries.
One should always prepare for a job interview (so that / as if) it can go as smoothly as possible.
(Although / Unless) the Internet allows people to get information quickly, the information is not always reliable.
People sometimes continue bad habits (if / even if) these habits have a negative effect on their lives.
```

```markdown
Teenagers often feel ==as if== they are alone. “好像”样貌
Sea levels will continue to rise ==unless== climate change is slowed down. “除非”让步
==Because== children do not get enough exercise, they are becoming unhealthier. “因为”原因
==Since== unemployment has increased in the past year, consumer spending has dropped greatly. “因为”原因
==When== athletes train too hard, they often get injuries. “时候”时间
One should always prepare for a job interview ==so that== it can go as smoothly as possible. “为了”目的
==Although== the Internet allows people to get information quickly, the information is not always reliable. “虽然”让步
People sometimes continue bad habits ==even if== these habits have a negative effect on their lives. “尽管”让步
```


## 注意

1. 状语从句在主句之前，需要逗号断开
2. 状语从句不能自己独立成句
3. 状语从句和主句绝大多数情况下都是完整句
4. 主从句只需要一个引导词

## 练习：改正状语从句中的错误

```markdown
When babies are first born they cry without shedding tears.
Though many people thought it was too dangerous Laura Dekker sailed around the world alone at the age of 16.
Because the rains in India this year started late farmers are concerned that they may have fewer crops this year.
Once the Titanic hit the iceberg it only took two hours and forty minutes to sink.

Although scientists researched the problem for many years. They were not able to find a solution.
Some people call a doctor whenever they feel sick. While others prefer to wait a day or two.
Because raccoons have been able to adjust to life in urban centers very well in many parts of the U.S.

They began planning for the building of the memorial after agreed on the design.
Although everyone is familiar with the yellow smiley face, its designer only $45 for it because he never obtained a patent.

Although many people think dogs and cats do not like each other, but that is not always the case.
While Queen Victoria became the queen of an empire of more than 400 million people when she was only 18 years old.
Although recent research on teens and video games has concerned many people because it appears that video games can be addictive for some teens.
Because the Atacama Desert in Chile is one of the driest places on Earth, so its soil has been compared to that on Mars.
```

```markdown
// 从句在前缺少逗号与主句断开
==When== babies are first born, they cry without shedding tears.
==Though== many people thought it was too dangerous, Laura Dekker sailed around the world alone at the age of 16.
==Because== the rains in India this year started late, farmers are concerned that they may have fewer crops this year.
==Once== the Titanic hit the iceberg, it only took two hours and forty minutes to sink.

// 从句自己独立成句
==Although== scientists researched the problem for many years, they were not able to find a solution.
Some people call a doctor whenever they feel sick, ==while== others prefer to wait a day or two.
Raccoons **have been able** to adjust to life _in urban centers_ _very well_ _in many parts of the U.S_.

// 状语从句和主句不完整
They began planning for the building of the memorial ==after== _they_ agreed on the design.

==Although== everyone is familiar with the yellow smiley face,
its designer **got/gained/earned/received** only $45 for it
==because== he never obtained a patent.

// 状语从句和主句引导词只需要一个
==Although== many people think dogs and cats do not like each other, that is not always the case.
Many people think dogs and cats do not like each other, ==but== that is not always the case.

Queen Victoria became the queen of an empire of more than 400 million people ==when== she was only 18 years old.

Recent research on teens and video games has concerned many people ==because== it appears that video games can be addictive for some teens. 原因：研究令很多人担忧，电子游戏令人上瘾（让步：研究令很多人担忧，但没事）

The Atacama Desert in Chile is one of the driest places on Earth, ==so== its soil has been compared to that on Mars.
==Because== the Atacama Desert in Chile is one of the driest places on Earth, its soil has been compared to that on Mars.
```
