# 从句作副词 = 副词从句（状语从句）

# 什么是状语从句 What is an Adverb Clause?

- 副词：给句子增加更多信息
- 英语句子：一组词，有完整含义，有主语，有动词
- 从句：由引导词开始的句子，不能脱离主句独立存在
- 状语从句就是一个从句作为副词，给句子增加更多信息
- 状语从句的引导词表示从句和主句的关系
- 状语从句可以放在主句前，也可以放在主句后，放在主句前需要逗号断开
- 状语从句就是副词从句

# 状语从句引导词表示从句和主句的六种关系

```markdown
We will have a meeting.
We will have a meeting when he comes. 时间
We will have a meeting because we have a problem. 原因
We will have a meeting so we can solve the problem. 目的
We will have a meeting if we have time. 条件
We will have a meeting although we do not have time. 让步、转折
We will have a meeting as if we are adults. 状样
```

## 练习：分辨状语从句的作用

```markdown
1. Some people believe that they sleep badly whenever there is a full moon. 时间
2. Though most scientists predicted heavy rainstorms, very little rain fell. 让步
3. The World Wildlife Fund is raising money so that it can help endangered species. 目的
4. Some people use this medicine for muscle injury since it can relieve pain. 原因
5. Although few animal species can speak, most animals communicate in some way. 让步
```

## 练习：选择正确的状语从句引导词

```markdown
1. Teenagers often feel (as if / although) they are alone.
2. Sea levels will continue to rise (while / unless) climate change is slowed down.
3. (Because / Before) children do not get enough exercise, they are becoming unhealthier.
4. (Once / Since) unemployment has increased in the past year, consumer spending has dropped greatly.
5. (When / Even if) athletes train too hard, they often get injuries.
6. One should always prepare for a job interview (so that / as if) it can go as smoothly as possible.
7. (Although / Unless) the Internet allows people to get information quickly, the information is not always reliable.
8. People sometimes continue bad habits (if / even if) these habits have a negative effect on their lives.
```

## 注意：

1、状语从句在主句之前，需要逗号断开
2、状语从句不能自己独立成句
3、状语从句和主句绝大多数情况下都是完整句
4、主从句只需要一个引导词

## 练习：改正状语从句中的错误

**从句在前缺少逗号与主句断开**

```markdown
1. When babies are first born they cry without shedding tears.
2. Though many people thought it was too dangerous, Laura Dekker sailed around the world alone at the age of 16.
3. Because the rains in India this year started late, farmers are concerned that they may have fewer crops this year.
4. Once the Titanic hit the iceberg, it only took two hours and forty minutes to sink.
```

**从句自己独立成句**

```markdown
5. Although scientists researched the problem for many years, they were not able to find a solution.
6. Some people call a doctor whenever they feel sick, while others prefer to wait a day or two.
7. Because Raccoons have been able to adjust to life in urban centers very well in many parts of the U.S.
```

**状语从句和主句不完整**

```markdown
8. They began planning for the building of the memorial after they agreed on the design.
9. Although everyone is familiar with the yellow smiley face, its designer got/gained/earned/received only $45 for it because he never obtained a patent.
```

**状语从句和主句引导词只需要一个**

```markdown
10. Although many people think dogs and cats do not like each other, but that is not always the case.
11. While Queen Victoria became the queen of an empire of more than 400 million people when she was only 18 years old.
12. Although recent research on teens and video games has concerned many people because it appears that video games can be addictive for some teens.
13. Because the Atacama Desert in Chile is one of the driest places on Earth, so its soil has been compared to that on Mars.
```
