# Question

```
A

My name is Barbara and I work at a department store. I (11)______ (work) there for one year when another Barbara joined the staff. Then I changed my name tag from "Barb" to "Barbie". (12)______ made me feel funny was how small kids talked about me. "Is she really Barbie?" they asked. I changed it at my other job, too and began answering the phone, "This is Barbie. How can I help you?" The callers have gotten used to that over time, ninety percent of (13)______ now respond with my name: "Barbie, can you tell me..." Pronouncing that long "e" sound forces your mouth into a smile, but I have found the smile is usually returned voluntarily.

B

It's said that for the Englishman, his house is his castle. However, this does not mean that his house is a beautiful palace that others (14)______ (invite) to see. For the British, the home is a place to protect oneself from the outside world. It's a private place in which he goes to hide away (15)______ the troubles of life. To the American, the home is an expression of (16)______ (he). Much money is often spent on each and every room (17)______ (create) the right "feel" according to the person's lifestyle. Therefore, he is happy to show his house to others.

C

Smoke jumpers are firefighters, trained to fight fires in places that fire engines can't reach. They travel in small planes and, (18)______ (use) a parachute, jump into remote wild areas to fight fires. Smoke jumpers have to respond quickly. While a fire is still small, the pilot (19)______ (drop) team members into the area as needed. Their first job may be to build a fire line to stop the fire from spreading. Water is sent down to them. Smoke jumpers must be (20)______ (high) trained, very experienced and extremely fit. Their job is very dangerous.
```

# Answer

## A

```
A

My name is Barbara and I work at a department store.
I (11)___had worked___ (work) there for one year when another Barbara joined the staff.
Then I changed my name tag from "Barb" to "Barbie".
(12)___What___ made me feel funny was how small kids talked about me.
"Is she really Barbie?" they asked.
I changed it at my other job, too and began answering the phone, "This is Barbie. How can I help you?"
The callers have gotten used to that over time, ninety percent of (13)___whom___ now respond with my name: "Barbie, can you tell me..."
Pronouncing that long "e" sound forces your mouth into a smile, but I have found the smile is usually returned voluntarily.
```

## 11 动词作谓语（过去完成/主动/单数）

```markdown
I **had worked** there _for one year_
==when== another Barbara **joined** the staff.

-========^------|----------------
1year joined now

谓语：过去之前/过去完成、主动、单数
```

```markdown
“for + 时间段” 不是现在完成的标志，可以用于任何时态

-===------------|----------------
----------------|------------===-
-------------===|----------------
-===^-----------|----------------

I **studied** in UK _for a year_. 过去（现在不在了）
I **will study** in UK _for a year_. 将来（将来才在）
I **have studied** in UK _for a year_. 过去到现在（现在还在）
I **had studied** in UK _for a year_ before I **found** a job. 过去之前
```

## 12 无提示词后面是句子：从句引导词（主x从x）

```markdown
==What== **made** me (to) feel funny
**was**
==how== small **kids** talked about me.

从句在句首，往后找到第二个谓语并且断开，前面是从句，后面是主句
空格在句首，特别注意大写字母

主x从x 主语从句
从句缺物what，从句缺人who；
从句缺状态how，从句缺位置where，从句缺时间when（考的很少）

I didn't know where I was.
I didn't know where the meeting was. // 上下文信息
I didn't know when the meeting was.
I didn't know how the meeting was.
I didn't know what the meeting was.
```

## 13 无提示词后面是句子：从句引导词（主v从x）

```markdown
The callers **have gotten** used to that over time,
~~ninety percent of~~ ==whom== now **respond** _with my name_:
"Barbie, can you tell me..."

// now（adv直接划掉）
// ninety percent of（adj直接无视）

无提示词空格前面看到“... of”直接划掉
many of / much of / some of / ninety percent of / a lot of ... “许多的”

主v从x 定语从句
人 that / who // 介词后whom 是whom的唯一考点
物 that / which
```

## 14 动词作谓语（一般现在/被动/复数）

```markdown

==that== his house **is** a beautiful palace
==that== others **are invited** to see.

谓语：一般现在、被动、复数
// invite后需要名词，但是没有名词，所以是被动

// 核心词汇
invite sb 邀请某人 正态度
invite sth 招致某事 中性偏负态度
invite - invitation
```

## 15 无提示词后面是短语：介词

```markdown
It **is** a private place
==in which== he goes to hide away _from the troubles_ _of life_.

介词重在积累
away from sb / sth 远离某人或某事
```

## 16 代词形式变化

```markdown
To the American, the home **is** an expression of himself.

介词 + 名词

主格、宾格、自己、某人的东西；某人的

his home - himself “他的家”对应“他自己”
```

## 17 动词作非谓语（主动/目的）

```markdown
Much money **is often spent** on (each and every) room _to create_ the right "feel"
_according to_ the person's lifestyle.

空格形容词？
空格动词非谓语？主动 // 后面有名词

// 非谓语和谓语打配合
to do 目的预期结果 // 描述谓语的目的
doing 同时“一边一边” // 和谓语同时

// 核心词汇
create v创造
creation n创造（过程）；n创造物（产物）
creator n创造者（人）
creative adj有创造力的
creatable adj可创造的
```

```
C

Smoke jumpers are firefighters, trained to fight fires in places that fire engines can't reach.
They travel in small planes and, (18)___using___ (use) a parachute, jump into remote wild areas to fight fires.
Smoke jumpers have to respond quickly.
While a fire is still small, the pilot (19)___drops / will drop___ (drop) team members into the area as needed.

```

## 18 动词作非谓语（主动/伴随）

```markdown
They **travel** _in small planes_ ==and==,
_using_ a parachute,
**jump** into remote wild areas _to fight_ fires.

and并列？
两个逗号之间的短语是插入语补充，插入语不纳入句子正式内容

非谓语
主动 // 空格后面有名词
非谓语和谓语的配合 “同时”

They **travel** _in small planes_ ==and==
**jump** into remote wild areas _to fight_ fires,
_using_ a parachute.
and连接两个动作：连续动作顺序
```

## 19 动词作谓语（一般现在/主动/单数）

```markdown
==While== a fire **is** still small,
the pilot **drops** team members _into the area_ _as needed_.

while “当...时” “尽管”

谓语：现在、主动、单数

// 核心词汇
drop - dropped - dropped - dropping
drop sth 掉落某物
drop sb 在某处送某人
```

## 20 词性变换（形容词变副词）

```markdown
Smoke jumpers **must be** _highly_ **trained**, very **experienced** and extremely **fit**.

形容词变副词

highly 高度地
```
