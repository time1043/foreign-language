# Write a Letter to Your past Self

Writing a letter to your past self can be a beautiful way to come to peace with your past, so that you can focus on the present moment.
Make sure to write it from a loving and non-judgmental space.
Take what you can learn and peacefully move on. It can go something like this:
Dear Past Self, I am sorry for all the mean things I said to you. I’m sorry for hating you, not believing in you and wishing that I’d rather be somebody else. I want to tell you that from now on I will always love and appreciate you. Thank you for being yourself. I love you.

# 给过去的自己写一封信

给过去的自己写一封信，是一种美好的与过去和解的方式，这样你才能专注于现在。
记得要充满爱意，不要随意评判。
吸取过去的经验，然后保持平和的心态继续前行。
你可以这样写：
亲爱的过去的自己：很抱歉我对你说的那些苛刻的话。很抱歉我曾经讨厌你，不相信你，甚至想要成为别人的样子。我想告诉你的是，从今往后，我会永远爱你，永远欣赏你。谢谢你坚持做自己。我爱你。
