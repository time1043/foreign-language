# You Are a Precious Human

You are the one and only, precious human.
There's no one like you.
No one has the perfect combination of your history, personality, and perspective.
That's tremendously valuable. No one can copy or reproduce who you are.
Are there any changes you want to see in the world?
Go ahead and spread your messages everywhere.
Don't worry if it's not original enough.
Why? First, even if it's already been said or done a million times, it hasn't been done with your personality.
Second, there are some people in the world who are destined to listen to your messages.

# 你是宝贵的存在

你是独一无二的、无比宝贵的存在。
没有人和你一样。
没有人和你有同样完美的经历、性格和视角。
这是无比宝贵的。没人有能够复制或重现你的一分一毫。
你是否希望看到这个世界的改变呢？
那就大胆地把你的想法说给所有人吧。
不用担心你的想法是否独到。
为什么呢？首先，即使已经有无数人说过或做过类似的事情，但是这件事还没有被你做过。
其次，这个世界上有些人命中注定要听到你的声音。
