# Expect to Make Mistakes

Expect to make some mistakes if you have incredibly high standards for yourself.
This is important, because in order to make mistakes, you have to take real actions.
This does not mean just researching. It means putting it to the market, making offers to your clients, and letting your voice be spoken into the world.
Don't even try to make it perfect from the start.
That only paralyzes you from moving forward.
Don't underestimate what you can do, either. So many things are possible if you just keep going.

# 做好犯错的准备

如果你对自己的要求很高，那就做好犯错误的准备。
这一点至关重要，因为犯错误的背后，是你真正在采取行动。
这不仅仅是前期的调研，而是将研究成果投入市场，向客户提出提案，并且让全世界听到你的声音。
不要试图从一开始就做到完美。
那只会让你停滞不前。
也不要低估自己的实力。只要坚持下去，一切都是有可能的。
