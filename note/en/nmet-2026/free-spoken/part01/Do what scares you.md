# Do what Scares You

Do you want to know how to overcome your fears? Just do it scared. What is the thing that you are most afraid to say or do? I challenge you to do it today.
This is how you live your life to the fullest.
If you have something that feels true to your heart, let it out.
Being scared should not be the sign to stop.
In fact, the bigger your goal is, the more you are supposed to feel scared.
If you feel totally comfortable with your dreams, set higher goals.
Speak your truth. Do what you're meant to do.
Be willing to feel scared.
Then, you will not be scared of that anymore.
You'll find another thing that makes you scared.
The more you repeat this process, the more you can create an amazing life.

# 做那些让你恐惧的事

想知道如何克服恐惧吗？那就带着恐惧去做吧。你最害怕说什么，或做什么？我要求你现在就去做。
这样的生活，才能过得毫无保留。
如果你心中有些真实的感受，那就说出来。
恐惧不应该成为停下的理由。
事实上，目标越大，你本来就越应该感到害怕。
如果你的梦想让你感觉非常舒适，那就给自己设一个更高的目标。
说出你的真心话。做你该去做的事。
心甘情愿地去面对自己的恐惧。
然后，你就不会再对它感到恐惧了。
你还会找到另一件让你恐惧的事。
这个过程重复的次数越多，你的生活就会越精彩。
