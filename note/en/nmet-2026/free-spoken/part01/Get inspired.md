# Get Inspired

When you see someone doing what you want to do, get inspired. Let that be a sign that you can also do what they are doing. Let them be a living example for you to learn and grow from.
Don't think successful people are different from you. They were just like you in the beginning. If they made it, you can also do it.
Pay close attention to how they trained and mastered their skills to create successful results, and follow their practices. Choose the path to improve yourself rather than to get jealous and stuck. Jealousy does not serve you any good.
No one took your place. No one is getting in your way. No one can drag you down. Get inspired by others' success. It will soon be yours.

# 获得激励

别人在做你想做的事情时，这就是对你的激励。把它看作一个信号：你也可以做到他们正在做的事情。把他们当作现实中的榜样，从中汲取经验，不断成长。
不要觉得成功的人与你是多么的不同。他们开始时也和你一样。如果他们能做到，你也可以。
仔细观察他们是如何通过训练掌握技能，从而取得成功的，然后学习他们的做法。选择能够提升自己的道路，而不是心存嫉妒，停滞不前。嫉妒的心态对于你没有任何意义。
没有人抢走你的位置。没有人阻碍你的前进。也没有人变成你的拖累。从他人的成功中获得激励。成功很快也会属于你。
