# Be Committed

You are always one commitment away from changing the whole trajectory of your life. Being committed means you do what you promised to do no matter what. If you make a commitment to put in the time and effort to constantly do something every day, no matter how trivial and basic it may look, it will sharpen your skills and shape you into the person you are meant to be in the future.
Maybe you want to be a generous person, but you don't help anyone because you are "too busy." But even after you get to the top, you'll always be "too busy" to make the time to give someone a hand.
If there's something you truly want, be committed to it.
Do it every day, no matter what.
That will make all the difference.

# 做出承诺

你和改变自己的人生轨迹之间，只差一个承诺。承诺意味着在任何情况下，你都会兑现自己说过的话。如果你承诺每天坚持投入时间和精力做某件事情，无论这件事看上去多么地微不足道、多么地轻而易举，它都会磨练你的技能，并且塑造未来的你。
也许你想要成为一个慷慨的人，却因为自己“太忙”而从未对别人伸出援手。但是即便你已经到达了人生的巅峰，你仍然会“太忙”，没有时间伸出援手。如果你真的想做这件事，那就对它做出承诺。
每天坚持，风雨无阻。
一切都将为之改变。
